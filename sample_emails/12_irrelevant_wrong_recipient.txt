BETTY'S FAMOUS APPLE PIE
Serves 8

Ingredients:
- 6 large Granny Smith apples, peeled and sliced
- 3/4 cup white sugar
- 1/4 cup brown sugar
- 1 teaspoon cinnamon
- 1/4 teaspoon nutmeg
- 2 tablespoons butter
- 2 pre-made pie crusts

Instructions:
1. Preheat oven to 425°F
2. Mix apples with sugars and spices
3. Pour into pie crust, dot with butter
4. Cover with second crust, crimp edges, cut vents
5. Bake 45-50 minutes until golden brown

The secret is the Granny Smith apples — they hold their shape and aren't too sweet!
